For You, Merlin

Read it slowly. Every word was chosen.

M

Merlin, I need you to know something. Not in a passing way — in a stop-what-you're-doing and actually listen way. You matter. Not because of what you do for people, but because of who you are when no one's watching. The quiet kind of kind. The loyal kind of loyal.

I've watched you give pieces of yourself to people who didn't always deserve it. I've watched you smile through things that would've broken most. And I've watched you stay soft in a world that keeps trying to make people hard. That takes a strength most people don't have a name for.

You have this thing where you make people feel seen. Like, actually seen. Not just heard. And I don't think you realize how rare that is, or how much it's changed me. The bad days got easier because you existed on them. The good days got better because you were there to laugh through them.

I'm not good at saying this out loud — so I'm saying it here instead, in a place you'll find when you least expect it. You are one of the best things that has ever happened to me. Not "a friend." One of the best things. Full stop.

Happy birthday, Merlin Sofia. I hope this year gives you back every bit of love you've poured into the world — ten times over. And I hope you know, on the days you forget, there's someone out here who is endlessly, quietly, permanently grateful that you exist.

— always yours
